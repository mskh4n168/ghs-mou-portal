Regional Memorandum of Understanding

Each region submits its signed Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) through this portal. A regional representative registers, fills in the MOU details on the site, downloads the completed MOU to sign, then uploads the signed hardcopy to the Legal Unit.

  1. Register with your details to create an account.
  2. Log in and complete the MOU details on the site.
  3. Download your completed MOU, sign it, then upload the signed copy (PDF).
  4. Submit — the Legal Unit is notified of your region's submission.
